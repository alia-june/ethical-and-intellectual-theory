## What was accepted

The eight G0 v0.4 theoretical axioms are G0-00, G0-00a, G0-00b, G0-00c, G0-01, G0-02, G0-03 and G0-07. Their full names, roles, and limits are in [FOUNDATIONAL_AXIOMS.md](FOUNDATIONAL_AXIOMS.md).

The **Gate Zero Modular Catalog** accepted **Modular Principle 0 — Explicit Adoption (`M0`)** and **23 of 27** selectable items in **Tranches 0–3**. Deferred were **Tranche 2, Item 03 — Provenance Continuity (`T2-03`)**, **Tranche 2, Item 06 — Pre-Copy Fallback (`T2-06`)**, **Tranche 3, Item 01 — Explicit Evidence State (`T3-01`)**, and **Tranche 3, Item 08 — Assurance Independence (`T3-08`)**. This means **available in a theoretical catalog**, not adopted by a project or proven to operate securely.

Fourteen enumerated claims remain **UNKNOWN**. No missing proof, undefined authority, disputed dependency, or unverifiable effect is silently marked as satisfied.

## Opt-in boundary

A reusable catalog may contain more protections than a particular project selects. An axiom is applicable only within its legitimate adopted scope, subject to independently binding restrictions. A missing dependency makes its claimed guarantee unestablished; it is not silently added to the project's active profile. An authorized project-profile revision does **not** unlock an external production gate. An AI may not grant itself rights through a research document.

## Scope and navigation

No source code, production controls, credentials, active access policies, or runtime enforcement mechanisms are published here. G0 v0.4 and G0-MOD v0.2 are **distinct theoretical records**, not claimed to be formally equivalent.
