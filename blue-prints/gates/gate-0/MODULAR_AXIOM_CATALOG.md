## Opt-in charter M0

A project does not adopt a protection by reading or linking this catalog. A scoped, authorized selection is required; missing prerequisites cannot be adopted by implication. Changes to optional profiles require independently valid revision authority and cannot remove binding external locks.

M1 (Dependency and Composition Integrity) is an umbrella, **not** a twenty-eighth atomic rule. T4–T7, proposed package equivalences, and implementation are deferred.

## Known deferrals

- **T2-03 Provenance Continuity:** The complete derivation and verification scheme is not established.
- **T2-06 Pre-Copy Fallback:** General sufficiency across failure and irreversible-effect models remains unestablished; PVI-08 stays as a preferred programming practice.
- **T3-01 Explicit Evidence State:** The proposed four-state encoding lacks fully specified admissibility/aggregation semantics.
- **T3-08 Assurance Independence:** Concrete assessor/source independence criteria remain unspecified.
