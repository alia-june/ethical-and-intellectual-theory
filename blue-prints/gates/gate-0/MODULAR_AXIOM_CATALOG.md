The catalog separates **library membership** from **project selection**, **selection** from **established prerequisites**, and **claimed guarantees** from **verified guarantees**. Dependencies may be logical, applicability, evidentiary or operational. Only justified proofs are entailments.

## Opt-in charter — Modular Principle 0: Explicit Adoption (`M0`)

A project does not adopt a protection by reading or linking this catalog. A scoped, authorized selection is required; missing prerequisites cannot be adopted by implication. Changes to optional profiles require independently valid revision authority and cannot remove binding external locks.

**Modular Umbrella 1 — Dependency and Composition Integrity (`M1`)** is not a twenty-eighth atomic rule. **Tranches 4–7** — Composition and Consequences; Temporal Validity; Independent Assurance; Containment and Recovery — remain deferred, as do package equivalences and implementation.

## Known deferrals

- **Tranche 2, Item 03 — Provenance Continuity (`T2-03`):** The complete derivation and verification scheme is not established.
- **Tranche 2, Item 06 — Pre-Copy Fallback (`T2-06`):** General sufficiency across failure and irreversible-effect models remains unestablished; PVI-08 stays as a preferred programming practice.
- **Tranche 3, Item 01 — Explicit Evidence State (`T3-01`):** The proposed four-state encoding lacks fully specified admissibility/aggregation semantics.
- **Tranche 3, Item 08 — Assurance Independence (`T3-08`):** Concrete assessor/source independence criteria remain unspecified.
