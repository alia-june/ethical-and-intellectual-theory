## Status and boundary

This page records the public relationship among the principal components of the Integrity of Legal Reasoning research. It does not publish the complete Bellotti Ladder examination procedure, the Implication engine specification, formal proof rules, case-level proof graphs, unpublished claim ledgers, constitutional-attestation measures, or operating instructions.

The research contains source-controlled methods, bounded findings, proposed theories, frozen designs, and unresolved questions with different evidentiary statuses. Inclusion here does not establish current law, judicial adoption, independent validation, implementation, or authority to act.

## Governing separations

The integrated research preserves the following distinctions:

```text
logical pass != constitutional pass
loss of support for Q != negation of Q
logical result != governance decision
formally operative != logically well-supported
not certain risk != no risk
citation identity != proposition identity
analytical relevance != binding authority
```

An independent later justification can become a new root for a proposition. Earlier ancestry then ceases to be load-bearing beyond that re-anchoring point. Re-anchoring must be demonstrated from actual independent support.

A shared case name or opposite outcomes do not establish contradiction. The compared proposition and its material predicates must be equivalent, and no valid distinction or independent support may account for the difference.

## Typed doctrinal material

The research treats a doctrine under examination as a typed set that may include holdings, necessary justifications, materially relevant implications, contradictions or dependency failures, and express boundaries. Inclusion in the analytical set requires treatment; it does not give every item identical precedential authority.

Historical witness-failure labels remain part of source provenance. The maintained public vocabulary is Condition Capture and Defeat:

| Historical label | Maintained diagnostic |
| --- | --- |
| Murdered Witness | Eliminated-condition capture |
| Dead Witness | Inherited-condition defeat |
| Undead Witness | Scope capture |
| Suicide | Reflexive condition defeat |

These labels describe research classifications. They do not independently establish overruling, invalidity, intent, misconduct, or a remedy.

## Bounded controls and findings

The archived campaign-finance research reports different control outcomes:

- A petition-and-association branch in which independent constitutional re-anchoring survived.
- A candidate-expenditure branch in which an express boundary was displaced and new grounds were supplied; the archive rejected automatic witness-failure classification.
- A source-reported Colorado II sequence used as an adjudication-lag control. The archive classified one premise-support failure and one later procedurally compelled reliance on formally binding degraded precedent. The classification attributes no deception or bad faith to the lower court.

These are archive-controlled research results. The primary judicial texts and the archive's legal characterizations require a separate external verification pass before they are represented as independently validated.

The archive does not establish a system-wide or cross-domain prevalence finding. Independent recurrence, a common mechanism, load-bearing significance, propagation, inadequate repair, and operational consequence remain necessary before an endemic or systemic classification could be supported.

## Precedent-health states

The integrated design distinguishes:

1. Binding authority with intact necessary reasoning.
2. Binding authority with degraded necessary reasoning.
3. Necessary reasoning rejected while the holding has not been formally overruled.
4. Formally overruled authority.

This model is a research proposal. It does not create a procedural entitlement or authorize a lower court to disregard controlling precedent.
