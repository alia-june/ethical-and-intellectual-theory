# Integrity of Legal Reasoning Public Glossary

Conceptual explanations and responsible disclosure | Expanded edition | 23 November 2026

This glossary accompanies the [public overview of Integrity of Legal Reasoning](https://github.com/alia-june/ethical-and-intellectual-theory/blob/main/legal-theory/integrity-of-legal-reasoning/README.md). It explains what the disclosed research names concern, why those subjects matter, and how firmly the work is established. It also defines ordinary terms used to describe evidence and research status.

The entries offer conceptual orientation rather than complete technical definitions. They do not provide diagnostic criteria, application procedures, formal models, causal chains, historical terminology crosswalks, worked cases, or novel deductions. Sensitive concepts receive a useful description of their subject without the detail needed to apply the underlying research. Some labels identify editorial groupings rather than independently defined theories.

Across the glossary, a research classification is distinct from a legal determination. Naming a concern does not establish misconduct, intention, liability, judicial adoption, or authority to act. A design description does not demonstrate an implemented capability. These general limits apply throughout, allowing individual entries to concentrate on meaning rather than repeating the same caution.

## Glossary

### Actionable Set

**Research concept.** A name for the material considered relevant to a proposed account of responsible adjudication. Its subject is the relationship between legal reasoning and the matters that deserve reasoned attention in a dispute. The word “actionable” expresses a research concern about relevance and responsibility; it does not mean that every matter identified creates a claim, remedy, or binding obligation. The complete proposed contents and selection criteria remain outside this public explanation.

### Adjudication Lag

**Research concept.** A subject concerning time in adjudication and the assessment of legal decisions. It asks readers to take the temporal setting of legal analysis seriously rather than treating every assessment as timeless. The research is interested in institutional responsibility, continuity, and change, but this glossary does not state the underlying explanatory account. The term alone establishes neither an improper delay nor a defect in an identified judgment, and it supplies no measure for assigning either conclusion.

### Adversarial Selection

**Research topic.** Evaluation in settings where the interests of participants may differ. The topic matters because confidence in an assessment depends partly on the circumstances in which its evidence was obtained and the purposes for which the assessment is used. This glossary does not reproduce a threat model or selection strategy. “Adversarial” describes a research setting; it is not a presumption that an unfamiliar submission, disagreement, or person is acting deceptively.

### Bellotti Ladder

**Legacy codename.** The former name of the research program now called Integrity of Legal Reasoning. It remains useful for identifying earlier archives, titles, and discussions accurately. The current name reflects the program's broader subject, while the legacy name preserves the continuity of its record. Historical appearances should be read in their source context. They do not establish a second current program, an additional legal doctrine, or a prediction about the development of constitutional rights.

### Closed Testing

**Research topic.** The scope of testing and the conclusions that evaluation can reasonably support. It belongs to the program's interest in research limits, confidence, and the adequacy of evidence. A successful evaluation is meaningful within the conditions actually examined; how broadly it can be understood remains a separate research question. This entry offers no testing protocol or account of a particular vulnerability. It also does not imply that repetition, controlled studies, or ordinary testing are inherently unreliable.

### Coherent Constitutional Erosion

**Research concept.** A named subject connecting questions of constitutional protection with the quality of legal reasoning. Its purpose is to support careful discussion of constitutional responsibility over time, while distinguishing a research concern from a demonstrated legal conclusion. The concept's explanatory model and assessment criteria are not supplied here. Its presence in the glossary does not establish that protection has diminished in an actual dispute, that a change is unconstitutional, or that institutions generally fail.

### Condition Capture and Defeat

**Author-defined research framework.** The currently maintained name of the program's legal-reasoning diagnostic work. Its broad subject is the treatment of supporting premises, qualifications, and scope in legal justification. The framework aims to improve the precision of research descriptions and the clarity of their limits. This public entry does not reproduce its categories, classification criteria, or examination procedure. The terminology is not an established judicial doctrine, and a research label cannot substitute for evidence about a particular argument.

### Confounding the Court

**Proposed research concept.** A title for related work on the integrity of institutional decision-making in a legal setting. The subject includes the quality of the material and reasoning on which institutions rely, together with the responsibilities associated with decisions that affect people. The separate synthesis remains research rather than an established cause of action. This glossary does not disclose its explanatory account or supply a way to classify a court, litigant, or proceeding under it.

### Constitutional Attestation

**Proposed design area.** Research concerning how a constitutional assessment might be represented clearly enough for its basis and limitations to be understood. The intended subject is accountable explanation, with appropriate attention to the authority and scope of the claim being made. The reviewed work does not establish a completed certification service. This glossary supplies no assessment criteria or technical design, and “attestation” must not be mistaken for a judicial ruling or an authoritative resolution of contested constitutional interpretation.

### Court as Constitutional Actor and Judicial Due Diligence

**Research inquiry.** The program's consideration of responsibility in the exercise of judicial power. It concerns the care, reasoning, and accountability associated with adjudication, especially where constitutional interests are at stake. The complete proposed duty and its supporting legal analysis remain matters for development. The terms therefore identify a subject of inquiry rather than declaring that every proposed obligation is already enforceable. Existing duties, procedural protections, and institutional limits require their own applicable legal authority.

### Defensive Reflexive Inversion

**Proposed research concept.** A name in the related institutional-theory work for a defensive line of inquiry. Its stated orientation is preservation of institutional integrity, accountable examination, and fair review. The detailed construction is withheld; the name should not be treated as describing a demonstrated capability. A defensive purpose is also not sufficient evidence that a response is effective, proportionate, or authorized. Those questions remain separate obligations for any later development or proposed real-world use.

### Designed-for-Rejection

**Proposed research concept.** A term concerning rejection in the related knowledge-examination work. It identifies a research interest in the circumstances under which material is assessed and refused, rather than treating every refusal as self-explanatory. The detailed proposed account is not disclosed here. Important causal and implementation questions remain unresolved. An observed rejection may be justified, and the name must not be used to infer deliberate design, coordination, manipulation, or motive from that observation alone.

### Domain-Relative Inference

**Research topic.** The relationship between reasoning and the scope within which a claim is made. The topic helps distinguish the quality of an argument from the breadth of the question it addresses. It matters when readers assess what a research result has, and has not, established. The formal account remains outside this glossary. Nothing in the term asserts that ordinary logical rules are generally invalid or that dependable inference is impossible.

### Dual-Use Authority

**Research inquiry.** A name within the program's study of precedent and the interpretation of legal authority. It concerns care in describing what authority contributes to an argument and how a research account represents its use. The technical meaning and comparison criteria are not given here. The phrase should not itself suggest misuse: interpretation can involve legitimate disagreement, and a research concern about an authority's use does not establish contradiction, deception, or professional misconduct.

### Extreme Possibility and Stopping-Principle Research

**Conceptual research.** Work concerned with the limits of arguments and the boundaries of claims. Such inquiry can clarify what a theory intends to cover and where its commitments remain uncertain. The detailed constructions and evaluation methods are withheld. A limiting question is not a forecast of what a court will do, and a hypothetical endpoint is not a description of present law. The research must remain proportionate and distinguish conceptual interest from evidence of an actual risk.

### Functional and Intrinsic Protection

**Research topic.** Terms used in the program's foundational consideration of constitutional protection and its justification. The subject is why a protection is argued to be appropriate, with attention to the language and assumptions used in that explanation. The detailed distinctions and applications remain outside this glossary. Their inclusion does not assign rights or legal status to a person or organization. It identifies a question for analysis rather than resolving every debate about constitutional protection.

### Implication

**Proposed tool name.** A formal-analysis project associated with the research program. Its intended purpose is to support disciplined examination of arguments and make analytical claims easier to assess. The source material describes a design ambition rather than a completed, independently validated implementation of the full program. This entry does not supply models, interfaces, rules, or operating instructions. A precise-looking output from any future tool would still require an adequate basis and a clear account of its limits.

### Independent Re-anchoring

**Research concept.** A name within the program's consideration of legal justification and the articulation of reasons. Its subject includes the adequacy of an explanation offered for a conclusion, and the care needed before characterizing that explanation as independent. The concept encourages precision in research descriptions without deciding the merits of any particular argument. Its technical definition and assessment criteria are withheld. Merely applying the label would not establish that a legal result is justified or correct.

### Independently Validated

**Evidence-status language.** Supported by evaluation beyond the original formulation, within a stated scope. A useful validation claim identifies what was assessed and why the evaluation deserves confidence. Publication, repetition, or inclusion in this glossary is not enough. Validation of one claim does not certify the whole research program.

### Integrity of Legal Reasoning

**Primary program name.** A developing research program concerning the quality, accountability, and constitutional responsibility of legal reasoning. It includes conceptual work, bounded analyses, proposals, and unresolved questions, with differing levels of maturity. Its purpose is to improve the care with which research questions and conclusions are expressed. The name identifies a subject of inquiry; it does not claim universal correctness, judicial adoption, or proof of a general institutional failure. Bellotti Ladder is its legacy codename.

### Justificatory Drift

**Research concept.** A term concerned with continuity and change in the reasons offered for legal conclusions. It invites careful attention to how legal explanations are understood over time, without assuming that change is necessarily improper. Its research value is in making questions about explanation more precise and open to discussion. In this program, it remains an analytical concept rather than an established judicial doctrine. This glossary does not provide criteria for diagnosing drift in a particular case.

### Knowledge Antithesis

**Author-defined research condition.** A current name within the related work on knowledge and reasoned examination. Its subject includes the quality of the conditions under which claims and theories are considered. The term expresses a research concern about examination, rather than a judgment that a particular belief or participant is defective. Its detailed definition and proposed relationships are not reproduced here. Particular instances require evidence; disagreement or an unwelcome conclusion is not by itself proof of manipulation.

### Lawful-Means Confounding

**Proposed research concept.** A name within the related institutional-theory work for a question involving legal permissibility and institutional integrity. It provides a subject for conceptual discussion without settling the legal status of actual conduct. The explanatory account and application criteria are withheld. The term does not itself establish that an act or outcome is lawful, unlawful, authorized, harmful, or sanctionable. Legal judgment still depends on the relevant facts, jurisdiction, timing, and applicable authority.

### Legal Crime

**Author-defined critical term.** Conduct judged morally or ethically repulsive while remaining legal. The term gives a name to a distinction between legal permissibility and ethical judgment; “crime” is not used here as an allegation of a criminal offense. This makes the critical vocabulary intelligible without settling whether any particular act meets its description. Both the ethical assessment and the claim of legality need their own basis. The term is research vocabulary rather than an established doctrine.

### Live Doctrinal Gestalt

**Research concept.** A name in the program's consideration of how legal doctrine is understood and represented in analysis. Its subject is the adequacy of an account of doctrine, rather than the mere presence of a citation or familiar title. The proposed internal structure is withheld. The concept remains part of the research framework and should not be read as enlarging the formal scope of precedent or making every observation about a doctrine legally binding.

### Multidimensional Attestation

**Proposed design area.** Work concerned with expressing analytical assessments clearly, including the scope and qualifications attached to them. The intended benefit is a more accountable presentation of conclusions, especially where a reader might otherwise misunderstand the strength of an assessment. The technical categories and their relationships are outside this glossary. No completed certification system is established by the name, and the research does not justify treating an assessment as a universal score of legal validity.

### Negative Finding

**Research-status language.** A result that does not establish the condition or claim examined within a stated inquiry. Such findings help constrain conclusions and deserve preservation alongside supportive results. They do not automatically prove that the condition is absent everywhere or that every related claim is false. Scope remains essential to their interpretation.

### Observer and Recovery Integrity

**Descriptive research grouping.** An editorial label used in the overview for related questions about examination, evaluation, and confidence in research practices. It draws attention to the responsibility involved in obtaining and maintaining a trustworthy account. The grouping is not presented as a separately validated theory or implemented product. Its underlying accounts and technical relationships remain outside this glossary. Readers should not infer that ordinary observation or corrective practices are inherently unsafe or ineffective.

### Open-World Uncertainty

**Research topic.** Uncertainty about the reach of a research account and the knowledge available for evaluating it. The subject matters because responsible conclusions depend on recognizing the limits of the evidence as well as its strengths. The program's particular treatment is withheld. Unfamiliarity does not, by itself, establish danger, manipulation, or wrongdoing. Nor does acknowledging uncertainty justify limitless investigation or abandoning proportionate standards of evidence and care.

### Poison Cache

**Named concept with unresolved content.** A title in the related knowledge-examination work whose complete independent definition has not been supplied. Its presence records a subject identified by the author, rather than demonstrating a technical object or established capability. Keeping the name visible can preserve the research record, provided its incompleteness is equally visible. This glossary does not infer storage behavior, persistence, construction, effects, or relationships from the ordinary meanings suggested by the words.

### Proper Construction Attack

**Author-defined research term.** An adversarial act characterized by the proposed lawfulness of its means. The phrase makes a distinction between the legal status of means and the other judgments that may be relevant to conduct. “Proper” is part of the chosen name, not a judicial endorsement. It does not settle purpose, consequences, authorization, or ethical acceptability, and it supplies no operational guidance. Applying it to an actual event would require separate legal and factual analysis.

### Proposed

**Research-status language.** Put forward for consideration, development, or evaluation. A proposal may have a clear formulation and supporting reasons while still requiring substantial work. The label leaves its outcome open: it is not a claim of adoption, implementation, effectiveness, or independent acceptance. Its value must be assessed on its actual merits.

### Purpose Capture

**Proposed research concept.** A name in the related institutional-theory work concerning the fidelity of decision-making to legitimate purposes. It provides a subject for inquiry into institutional responsibility without proving anything about a specific actor's intentions. The underlying explanatory account and application criteria are withheld. A disagreement about an outcome does not establish that a purpose was captured. Evidence, competing explanations, and the distinction between research interpretation and legal responsibility remain important.

### Reciprocal Justification

**Research concept.** A name within the program's consideration of argumentative responsibility and the standards used to defend legal claims. Its broad purpose is to support careful discussion of how reasons are offered and evaluated. The detailed method and its proposed implications are not reproduced here. It is not asserted as a universal legal requirement, and the name supplies no procedure for defeating an argument. It remains a subject for conceptual and legal development.

### Recursive Poisoning

**Proposed examination problem.** A name in the related knowledge work concerning the responsibilities and difficulties associated with examining affected material. It marks a research question about inquiry rather than a recommendation to expose a person or system to harmful content. Its detailed account and any relationship to other named concepts are withheld. The source does not establish that exposure is universally necessary, useful, harmless, or authorized, and no operational capability is claimed.

### Recursive Precedent and Dependency

**Research topic.** The examination of legal reasoning that draws on earlier authority. Its purpose is to support precise descriptions of the reasons and sources presented in legal argument, with appropriate care about what the evidence establishes. The underlying model, tracing method, and assessment criteria are withheld. Citation alone should not be treated as proof of whatever a research interpretation claims. This entry does not provide a diagnostic procedure or decide the merits of a particular precedent.

### Responsible Disclosure

**Publication practice.** Selecting the content appropriate to a stated audience and purpose while explaining the limits of what is released. The practice combines restraint about detail with restraint about claims: readers should not be asked to accept withheld evidence as proof. Limited disclosure neither guarantees confidentiality nor establishes effectiveness. Further publication requires its own considered decision.

### Reverse-Human Test

**Conceptual research name.** A title used in the program's limiting-case consideration of human status and rights. Its role is to identify a philosophical and constitutional question for careful examination, rather than to propose a change in anyone's treatment. The construction, deductions, and applications are withheld. The term does not assert that a hypothetical endpoint is lawful, predicted, or present in existing doctrine. Human dignity and accurate distinctions between research and legal authority remain essential.

### Rights-Impact Review

**Proposed design area.** Work concerned with making the interests of affected people visible in the discussion of legal and analytical assessments. Its purpose is to support responsible attention to the stakes of a claim, with suitable evidence and explanation. The particular review methods and technical content are withheld. The name does not establish an implemented process, a validated measure, an infringement of rights, or an entitlement to a specified outcome. Those conclusions require additional grounds.

### Risk and Uncertainty

**Research topic.** Responsible assessment where knowledge is incomplete and consequences matter. It concerns the care with which confidence, concern, and limitations are expressed, and the need for proportionate claims. The program's specific account is not supplied here. A possibility must not be presented as certainty, and uncertainty must not become a presumption against a person or institution. A meaningful research discussion remains open to contrary evidence and to conclusions that reduce an initial concern.

### Supported

**Evidence-status language.** Backed to a stated extent by identified reasons or evidence. The description should be accompanied by enough context to understand what the support covers and where it is limited. Support may be partial or contested. It is not automatically equivalent to independent validation, general applicability, judicial adoption, or authority to act.

### Systemic Dependency Weakness

**Unproven broad research hypothesis.** A title for a wider institutional question in the program. It identifies an ambition to examine the breadth and significance of a concern without presuming that the concern is general. The explanatory model and assessment methods are withheld. The program does not establish a demonstrated defect across law merely by naming this hypothesis. Broad claims would require evidence appropriate to their breadth, including serious consideration of contrary findings and institutional resilience.

### Trajectory Review

**Proposed design area.** Work concerning the assessment of legal developments over time. Its intended purpose is to make temporal scope and the basis of an interpretation clearer in responsible research. The particular methods, technical relationships, and measures are withheld. The name does not establish that a trend has been demonstrated, that a sequence of decisions is legally defective, or that a proposed review tool has been validated. Interpretation must remain distinct from demonstrated results.

### Transformative Observation

**Research topic.** The relationship between observation, representation, and the knowledge available for assessment. Its purpose is to encourage care about the basis on which an account is presented as dependable. The program's explanatory treatment is withheld. The topic should not be taken as a universal claim that observation is harmful, all records are unreliable, or trustworthy knowledge is impossible. The scope and support of any particular research claim remain matters for evidence and scrutiny.

### Unresolved

**Research-status language.** Not settled by the available formulation or evidence. A matter may remain unresolved because a definition is incomplete, interpretations differ, validation is missing, or a question has not been answered. The label preserves that state honestly. It is neither confirmation of a claim nor proof of its opposite, and it should not be replaced by an invented answer.

## Source basis and limits

The source basis is the public Integrity of Legal Reasoning overview and the previously reviewed research definitions. These expanded entries provide editorial explanations of subject, purpose, and status; they do not amend the underlying definitions or add new theoretical claims. The glossary makes no current-law, novelty, implementation, or effectiveness determination.

The entries were reviewed together as well as individually. Explanatory detail was limited where a fuller account would disclose a method or allow separate entries to become a reconstruction of withheld research. The glossary has no links to detailed mechanisms or unpublished research materials; its navigation leads only to the approved public overview.

Some concepts remain incompletely formulated, while other labels collect related topics for readability. Those differences are retained rather than flattened into one level of maturity. Publication and fuller explanation do not resolve the remaining research obligations.

**Attribution:** Human-directed research and conceptual development; AI-assisted research organization and editorial formulation under human instruction.
