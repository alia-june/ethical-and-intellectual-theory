# Integrity of Legal Reasoning Public Glossary

Reader explanations and responsible disclosure | 23 November 2026

This glossary accompanies the [public overview of Integrity of Legal Reasoning](https://github.com/alia-june/ethical-and-intellectual-theory/blob/main/legal-theory/integrity-of-legal-reasoning/README.md). It explains the use and status of names already disclosed there, together with essential research language.

These are reader explanations, not complete technical definitions. Sensitive entries identify a subject and its limits without supplying the material needed to apply the research. The glossary omits diagnostic subpatterns, classification criteria, procedures, formal models, causal relationships, worked cases, and new deductions. Its omissions limit what readers can independently evaluate; they do not establish that withheld claims are correct.

Named concepts do not all have the same maturity. A research label does not establish a legal doctrine, an allegation, a functioning tool, or permission to act. Descriptive explanations below do not amend the underlying source definitions. Where a public explanation cannot responsibly be more specific, the limitation is stated rather than filled with an invented definition.

## Glossary

### Actionable Set

**Research term.** A name used in the program's consideration of legal reasoning and adjudicative responsibility. Its technical content is outside this glossary. “Actionable” does not establish a cause of action, an enforceable duty, or a right to a remedy.

### Adjudication Lag

**Research term.** A named area of inquiry concerning the assessment of adjudication over time. This entry does not disclose its explanatory account, identify a defective judgment, or establish delay, wrongdoing, or legal entitlement in a particular matter.

### Adversarial Selection

**Research topic.** An area of inquiry concerning evaluation in adversarial settings. Its mention does not imply that a particular person, submission, or unfamiliar event is adversarial. The relevant assumptions and technical treatment are not reproduced here.

### Bellotti Ladder

**Legacy codename.** The former name of the research program now called Integrity of Legal Reasoning. The legacy name remains in historical sources and titles. Those appearances identify the research record rather than a second current program or a new legal doctrine.

### Closed Testing

**Research topic.** A name used in discussion of evaluation and the limits of research evidence. This glossary supplies no testing method or account of a particular limitation. It does not claim that repeated testing is inherently unreliable.

### Coherent Constitutional Erosion

**Research term.** A named subject in the program's broader constitutional inquiry. The underlying account and assessment criteria are withheld. Naming it does not establish that an actual legal development is unconstitutional or that an institution has generally failed.

### Condition Capture and Defeat

**Author-defined framework.** The currently maintained name of a legal-reasoning diagnostic framework within the research. Its categories, criteria, and examination procedure are outside this glossary. The name does not establish misconduct, intention, legal invalidity, or judicial adoption.

### Confounding the Court

**Proposed research concept.** A title for related institutional research presented in a separate synthesis. Its analytical content is not reproduced here. It is not asserted as an established cause of action or a finding about an identified court or litigant.

### Constitutional Attestation

**Design area.** A name within the program's proposed analytical-tool research. This entry does not supply certification criteria or establish an implemented service. “Attestation” is not a judicial decision, binding constitutional interpretation, or guarantee of correctness.

### Court as Constitutional Actor and Judicial Due Diligence

**Research inquiry.** Names for the program's consideration of judicial responsibility. The complete thesis and supporting analysis remain outside this glossary. They should not be read as establishing a particular unrecognized duty or directing a court or litigant to act.

### Defensive Reflexive Inversion

**Proposed research concept.** A name in the related institutional-theory work. The defensive designation expresses its research orientation; it does not establish effectiveness, authorization, or an implemented capability. This glossary does not explain its operation or proposed application.

### Designed-for-Rejection

**Proposed research concept.** A name used in the related knowledge-examination work. Its explanatory content is withheld. An observed rejection does not, by itself, establish deliberate construction, manipulation, coordination, or an individual's intention. Important development questions remain unresolved.

### Domain-Relative Inference

**Research topic.** A name used in the program's discussion of reasoning and research scope. The formal treatment is not reproduced. The term does not assert that ordinary logical rules are generally invalid or that reliable inference is impossible.

### Dual-Use Authority

**Research inquiry.** A name for part of the program's precedent research. Its particular analytical meaning is outside this glossary. The name alone does not identify a contradiction, misconduct, or an improper use of authority in a case.

### Extreme Possibility and Stopping-Principle Research

**Conceptual research.** Names used for the program's consideration of limiting questions. The underlying constructions and their evaluation are not disclosed here. A hypothetical endpoint is not a prediction, a statement of current law, or an instruction for action.

### Functional and Intrinsic Protection

**Research topic.** Terms appearing in the program's foundational constitutional inquiry. This glossary does not reproduce the proposed distinctions or their applications. Their inclusion does not determine the rights or legal status of a particular person or organization.

### Implication

**Proposed tool name.** The title of a formal-analysis project described in the research. It does not identify a completed, independently validated implementation of the full program. No model, interface, rule system, or operating procedure is supplied here.

### Independent Re-anchoring

**Research term.** A name within the program's consideration of legal justification. Its technical definition and evaluation criteria are outside this glossary. It does not, by its use alone, establish that an argument is adequate or a legal result is correct.

### Independently Validated

**Evidence-status language.** A claim supported by evaluation beyond its original formulation, within a stated scope. Independence and adequacy require explanation. Publication, repetition, or appearance in this glossary does not itself establish validation, and validation of one claim does not certify the whole program.

### Integrity of Legal Reasoning

**Primary program name.** A developing research program concerning the quality, accountability, and constitutional responsibility of legal reasoning. It includes concepts, bounded analyses, proposals, and unresolved questions. Its name expresses a subject of inquiry rather than a claim of universal correctness.

### Justificatory Drift

**Research term.** A name within the program's inquiry into legal explanation and justification. This entry withholds its explanatory account and diagnostic use. Calling a concern by this name does not establish that a particular precedent or argument is defective.

### Knowledge Antithesis

**Author-defined research condition.** A current name within the related knowledge-examination work. Its detailed definition and proposed relationships are not reproduced here. Use of the term is not proof of falsehood, manipulation, intention, or an actual compromised process.

### Lawful-Means Confounding

**Proposed research concept.** A name in the related institutional-theory synthesis. The analytical account is withheld. The term does not establish that any actual act is lawful, unlawful, authorized, sanctionable, or harmful. Those determinations require separate evidence and applicable law.

### Legal Crime

**Author-defined critical term.** A name used to distinguish ethical criticism from legal assessment. It is not an allegation of a criminal offense. Its use cannot establish the legality of an act or replace examination of the relevant facts and jurisdiction.

### Live Doctrinal Gestalt

**Research term.** A name for part of the program's legal-reasoning inquiry. Its internal structure is not reproduced here. It should not be read as enlarging the formal scope of precedent or making every research observation legally binding.

### Multidimensional Attestation

**Design area.** A name within the proposed analytical-tool work. The categories, technical relationships, and assessment methods are outside this glossary. It does not establish a completed certification system, a universal validity score, or a guarantee about legal conclusions.

### Negative Finding

**Research-status language.** A result that does not establish the condition or claim examined within the inquiry's stated scope. It is part of the evidence record. It does not automatically prove that the condition is absent everywhere or that all related claims are false.

### Observer and Recovery Integrity

**Descriptive research grouping.** A convenient label in the overview for related examination and evaluation topics. It is not asserted as a separately validated doctrine or implemented product. This glossary does not provide the underlying accounts or technical relationships.

### Open-World Uncertainty

**Research topic.** A name within the related work on the limits of knowledge and evaluation. Its specific treatment is withheld. Uncertainty or unfamiliarity alone is not evidence of danger, manipulation, wrongdoing, or a need for unlimited investigation.

### Poison Cache

**Named concept with unresolved content.** The source material does not supply a complete independent definition or demonstrated implementation. This glossary preserves that gap. The name should not be expanded into an assumed storage system, technical behavior, or verified capability.

### Proper Construction Attack

**Author-defined research term.** A name in the related legal-theory work concerning legal and ethical assessment. “Proper” is part of the chosen name, not judicial approval. The term provides no authorization, operational guidance, or finding about an identified person's conduct.

### Proposed

**Research-status language.** Put forward for consideration, development, or evaluation. A proposal may be reasoned and worth examining while remaining unvalidated. The label does not imply adoption, implementation, effectiveness, or agreement by an independent authority.

### Purpose Capture

**Proposed research concept.** A name in the related institutional-theory work. Its explanatory account and application criteria are withheld. The label does not by itself establish another person's purpose, an institutional failure, deliberate manipulation, or legal responsibility.

### Reciprocal Justification

**Research term.** A name within the program's discussion of argument and justification. The method and its proposed implications are outside this glossary. Its presence does not establish a universal legal requirement or supply a procedure for challenging an argument.

### Recursive Poisoning

**Proposed examination problem.** A name in the related knowledge-examination work. The underlying account is withheld. The term does not establish that exposure to affected material is necessary, safe, useful, or authorized, and it is not a recommendation to undertake exposure.

### Recursive Precedent and Dependency

**Research topic.** A name within the program's analysis of legal reasoning. The underlying model, tracing method, and criteria are not supplied here. The title does not establish that a particular citation or precedent creates the relationship suggested in a research claim.

### Responsible Disclosure

**Publication practice.** Deliberately limiting the content released for a stated purpose while being candid about what the release can demonstrate. Limited disclosure does not prove effectiveness, guarantee confidentiality, or make omitted evidence available for independent evaluation.

### Reverse-Human Test

**Conceptual research name.** A title used in the program's limiting-case work. The construction and deductions are not reproduced. It does not assert that a hypothetical endpoint is lawful, predicted, present in existing doctrine, or appropriate to enact.

### Rights-Impact Review

**Design area.** A name in the program's proposed review and attestation work. Its assessment methods are not supplied. It does not establish an implemented review process, a finding that rights have been infringed, or an entitlement to a particular legal outcome.

### Risk and Uncertainty

**Research topic.** A named area concerned with responsible assessment under incomplete evidence. Its specific framework is not disclosed here. A concern must not be presented as certainty, and uncertainty must not become a presumption against a person or institution.

### Supported

**Evidence-status language.** Backed to a stated extent by identified reasons or evidence. The description must be read with its scope and qualifications. Support can be partial or contested; it does not automatically establish independent validation, generality, or legal authority.

### Systemic Dependency Weakness

**Unproven broad research hypothesis.** A named area of inquiry in the program. This glossary does not disclose its explanatory model or assert a demonstrated general defect across law. The breadth of the name must not be mistaken for the breadth of established evidence.

### Trajectory Review

**Design area.** A name within the proposed review and attestation work. Its methods and technical content are withheld. Its inclusion does not establish a validated assessment tool, a demonstrated trend, or a legal conclusion about a sequence of decisions.

### Transformative Observation

**Research topic.** A name in the related examination and knowledge work. Its explanatory treatment is outside this glossary. The term is not a claim that observation is always harmful, that all records are unreliable, or that trustworthy knowledge is impossible.

### Unresolved

**Research-status language.** Not settled by the available formulation or evidence. An unresolved matter may involve an incomplete definition, disputed interpretation, missing validation, or an unanswered question. The label is neither proof of a claim nor proof of its opposite.

## Source basis and publication limits

The basis is the published Integrity of Legal Reasoning overview and the previously reviewed research definitions. This glossary adds reader explanations, not new theory, current-law determinations, or validation claims. Some grouped labels in the overview are descriptive editorial names rather than independently defined frameworks; their entries identify that limitation.

The entries were reviewed both individually and together for consistency with the overview's disclosure boundary. No glossary can make a broader promise that related information is unavailable elsewhere. Further disclosure requires a separate decision; the detailed reports, registers, source excerpts, and historical records are not incorporated into this publication.
