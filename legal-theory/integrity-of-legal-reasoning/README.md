## Purpose and disclosure boundary

Integrity of Legal Reasoning is the primary name of the developing research program concerned with the quality, accountability, and constitutional responsibility of legal reasoning. Bellotti Ladder is preserved within that program as a proposition-level recursive precedent and justificatory-dependency audit. Some historical materials used Bellotti Ladder as a broader project name; this overview retains that history without discarding the method's distinct function. The program's starting subject is corporate constitutional protection. Its broader interests include the evaluation of legal arguments, the responsible use of analytical tools, and the standards needed before research claims are presented as established conclusions.

This overview covers the named theory families represented in the reviewed research. It describes their purposes, maturity, limitations, and safeguards. It intentionally withholds underlying mechanisms, diagnostic procedures, detailed applications, formal models, and new deductions. The detailed evidence records are separate research materials and are not incorporated by reference into this document.

This is a statement of a research program, not a complete technical disclosure. Readers should not treat withheld analysis as proof of a public claim. Where this overview does not provide enough evidence for independent evaluation, the appropriate status is limited or unresolved. Responsible restraint in disclosure must be accompanied by restraint in claims.

## Integrated component relationships

The [integrated architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) distinguishes the umbrella program from its methods, diagnostic vocabulary, proposed formal tool, attestation designs, and governance layer. Bellotti Ladder traces typed propositions and necessary justificatory dependencies through precedent. Condition Capture and Defeat supplies the maintained public diagnostic vocabulary. Implication is a separate consequence-neutral specification concerned with truth, contradiction, support, authority, analogy, risk, and hypothetical possibility. Multidimensional and Constitutional Attestation preserve separate constitutional, natural-person-rights, trajectory, and governance assessments.

These relationships are source-controlled research classifications. They do not establish that the complete methods have been independently validated or implemented.

## Constitutional reasoning as the principal subject

The Integrity of Legal Reasoning research concerns the careful study of constitutional reasoning in a defined area of law. It seeks to improve the precision with which a research claim is stated, attributed, examined, and qualified. Its name does not imply that constitutional protections inevitably develop in a particular direction or that a preferred legal outcome has been established.

The associated work includes Justificatory Drift, Recursive Precedent and Dependency, Independent Re-anchoring, the Live Doctrinal Gestalt, the Actionable Set, and proposed judicial due-diligence questions. These names identify distinct parts of the research agenda. Their inclusion here does not disclose their internal operation or claim that courts have adopted the complete framework.

The Three-Domain Model, Functional and Intrinsic Protection research, and the Court as Constitutional Actor inquiry address foundational questions that require careful treatment of legal and normative language. They remain subjects for reasoned development. A conceptual distinction should not be presented as a finding about the rights, obligations, or legal status of any particular person or organization.

## Current diagnostic terminology

Condition Capture and Defeat is the current name of the program's maintained legal-reasoning diagnostic framework. Earlier source materials use historical terminology. Those historical records should remain accurately attributed while current discussion identifies the presently maintained vocabulary.

This overview does not reproduce the diagnostic patterns, their correspondence with earlier terms, or the examination procedure. The public purpose of identifying the framework is to make its existence and research status clear without offering a method for applying it.

The framework is author-defined research terminology. A label assigned within research does not establish a judicial finding, an offense, misconduct, or an available remedy. Particular applications require evidence, relevant legal authority, appropriate expertise, and fair consideration of disagreement. Uncertainty must remain visible rather than being converted into the appearance of a settled determination.

## Limiting cases and research discipline

Extreme Possibility and Stopping-Principle research, the Reverse-Human Test, Reciprocal Justification, and the Dual-Use Authority inquiry form part of the program's conceptual work. Their role in this overview is to identify areas of inquiry and their limits. No hypothetical endpoint is presented here as a prediction, an existing legal condition, or an instruction for action.

Risk and Uncertainty research also belongs to this agenda. Responsible inquiry requires a proportionate response to a supported concern. Uncertainty alone does not justify a conclusion, unlimited investigation, or a presumption against a person. The research must remain open to correction, alternative explanations, and findings that narrow or reject an initial concern.

Negative findings are part of that discipline. A serious research program must retain results that do not support its initial expectations. It should not turn every ambiguity into confirmation or treat repeated restatement as additional evidence. The credibility of the work depends on its willingness to preserve limits and revise claims.

## Broader institutional questions

Adjudication Lag, Coherent Constitutional Erosion, and Systemic Dependency Weakness are named research areas within the broader program. They raise questions about the assessment of legal institutions and the strength of evidence required for broad conclusions. This overview does not state their underlying explanatory claims or reproduce particular applications.

No finding of general failure across the legal system is asserted here. A bounded study cannot establish prevalence throughout unrelated fields. The program requires independent examination before broader descriptions can be justified, and it must account for evidence of institutional resilience as well as evidence of difficulty.

Confounding the Court, Purpose Capture, Lawful-Means Confounding, and Defensive Reflexive Inversion are related research concepts considered in a separate synthesis. They remain proposals rather than established legal doctrines or demonstrated operational capabilities. Their inclusion identifies the breadth of the research interests; it does not imply that they are identical to Integrity of Legal Reasoning or that all related concepts have been integrated into one validated theory.

## Legal and ethical distinctions

Legal Crime and Proper Construction Attack are author-defined terms within the related legal-theory work. They support discussion of legal and ethical questions at a conceptual level. Their names are not allegations about an identified person, a judicial endorsement, or evidence that any particular act has been legally evaluated.

Legal permissibility, ethical acceptability, authorization, and responsibility require their own examination. Research terminology cannot supply permission to act or substitute for applicable law. It must not be used to suggest that a critic has established wrongdoing merely by choosing a forceful term.

Any later use involving an actual dispute would require attention to facts, jurisdiction, timing, professional obligations, and procedural fairness. This overview makes no recommendation about litigation, enforcement, investigation, or action against a person or institution.

## Knowledge and examination research

Knowledge Antithesis, Poison Cache, Designed-for-Rejection, and Recursive Poisoning are additional named concepts in the reviewed legal-theory material. This overview acknowledges them without explaining their proposed mechanisms or asserting that they form a single causal system.

Their development is uneven. Some have supplied definitions; some relationships and important details remain unresolved. An evocative name is not evidence that a mechanism exists, has been implemented, or works as expected. Where the source does not provide an answer, the public account should not fill the gap through speculation.

The related work on Open-World Uncertainty, Closed Testing, Domain-Relative Inference, Transformative Observation, Adversarial Selection, and Observer and Recovery Integrity likewise remains subject to evidence and scope. Its inclusion does not imply that unfamiliar information is dangerous, that observation is generally unreliable, or that ordinary examination should be abandoned. Such broad claims would exceed the responsible status of the research.

## Formal tools and attestation research

Implication is the name of a proposed consequence-neutral formal-analysis tool. Its specification separates admitted truth and falsity, contradiction, known proof support, independent surviving support, operative legal authority, analogy, risk, and hypothetical possibility. Loss of all examined support for a proposition does not by itself prove the proposition false. Multidimensional Attestation, Constitutional Attestation, Rights-Impact Review, and Trajectory Review are associated frozen design areas. This overview names these projects and their public relationships without supplying formal rules, case-level proof graphs, operating procedures, implementation requirements, or the withheld engine specification.

The reviewed material does not establish a completed, independently validated system implementing the full program. Design requirements, recorded intentions, and frozen specifications must not be advertised as demonstrated capabilities. Software correctness, legal interpretation, and institutional responsibility each require evidence appropriate to the claim being made.

Human judgment and accountability remain necessary. A research tool should not be presented as an authority that resolves contested constitutional questions merely because its output appears precise. A public description must make the limits of an analytical system at least as clear as its intended benefits.

## Safeguards for responsible development

The program should preserve source attribution, correction history, uncertainty, disagreement, and the distinction between an author's proposal and independent validation. It should avoid unsupported accusations and outcome-driven selection of evidence. Appropriate independent review should precede stronger claims about effectiveness, generality, or legal consequences.

Legitimate advocacy, access to courts, due process, confidentiality, and the rights of affected people must remain central constraints. Research classifications should not become shortcuts around those protections. Any proposed safeguard must itself be open to criticism and correction.

Disclosure is a separate responsibility. This document excludes details outside its approved purpose. It does not promise that information is unavailable elsewhere or claim that limited publication establishes confidentiality. Further disclosure would require its own decision about purpose, audience, evidence, and consequences.

## Research standing and remaining obligations

Integrity of Legal Reasoning should presently be understood as a structured research program containing developed concepts, bounded analyses, proposals, and unresolved questions. Its related projects do not all have the same maturity. This overview makes no claim of comprehensive novelty, universal correctness, judicial adoption, or demonstrated technical effectiveness.

Further work must establish clear scope, reproducible support where disclosure permits, independent scrutiny, meaningful counterevidence, and proportionate claims. Historical source checks must not be represented as fresh verification. Any future public conclusion should identify what was actually examined and what remains unknown.

The basis for this overview is the baseline and successor research dated August 2026 preserved under the legacy codename Bellotti Ladder, the Constitutional Hardening Redux freeze, the current Alia-June legal-theory definitions reviewed on 23 November 2026, and the dated Confounding the Court synthesis. Detailed source locations and research assessments are retained separately. Their omission here limits what this overview can demonstrate; it does not enlarge the authority of its conclusions.
