# Designed-for-Rejection

**Canonical term:** Designed-for-Rejection  
**Former research label:** ICHK (historical repository spelling; expansion unresolved)

## Author-directed definition

**Designed-for-Rejection** names a proposed mechanism or construction by which material is made to be rejected.

This describes a **rejection-oriented mechanism**, not a synonym for corrupted knowledge and not proof of any actual person's intention. The term “designed” identifies the hypothesized function of the construction; whether a particular instance was deliberate, effective, or caused rejection is a separate evidentiary question.

## Scope and boundaries

The original research formulation described “the mechanism by which something is poisoned for rejection.” The current formulation makes the proposed function more legible without assuming that corruption is the sole possible cause or that every instance of rejection is engineered.

Do not infer a rejection-oriented design from rejection alone. An item might be rejected for valid reasons, and the fact of rejection does not establish manipulation, intent, coordination, or a wrongful act.

The author has not supplied an implementation, operational procedure, success criterion, or complete causal account. These remain **unresolved**. This research entry gives no instructions for inducing rejection or manipulating evidence.
