# Knowledge Corruption

**Canonical term:** Knowledge Corruption  
**Former research label:** Anti-Knowledge (retained only for historical reference)

## Author-directed definition

**Knowledge Corruption** names a condition in which material presented or treated as knowledge has been compromised in relation to **reasoned inspection** or to the **adoption or repudiation of a theory**.

The reframe names the defect in the epistemic material or its treatment rather than asserting that the material belongs to a separate category of “anti-knowledge.” The corruption may affect a claim, its supporting evidence, its presentation, or the process by which it is examined. These are conceptual possibilities, **not proven mechanisms or events**.

## Scope and boundaries

The classification alone does **not** establish that the claim is false, the source is untrustworthy in all respects, the corruption was intentional, or anyone committed an offense. A defensible assessment requires a stated condition, material evidence, defined scope, and examination of what, if anything, was actually compromised.

Knowledge corruption is a **condition**, not necessarily an attack, a causal explanation, or proof of any actor's motive. It can be relevant to acceptance and rejection without presuming that the goal was rejection.

## Relationships

- **[Knowledge Antithesis](../knowledge-antithesis/README.md):** The author-named antithetic condition to Knowledge Corruption, concerning preservation of reasoned inspectability and corrigibility; not an automatic guarantee of factual truth.
- **[Poison Cache](../poison-cache/README.md):** The author identifies Knowledge Corruption as its **root**. No detailed cache mechanism follows from that root relationship alone.
- **[Designed-for-Rejection](../designed-for-rejection/README.md):** Knowledge Corruption **may participate** in a rejection-oriented design or mechanism, but the two concepts are not equivalent and the relationship is not mandatory.
- **[Recursive Poisoning](../recursive-poisoning/README.md):** The proposed examination problem concerns encountering compromised material while attempting to understand it. Such exposure is not automatically necessary, safe, or sufficient.
