# Recursive Poisoning

**Historical author-supplied definition (terminology updated):** The **need to ingest the poison in order to understand material knowledge or Knowledge Corruption**.

## Scope

**Recursive Poisoning** names the author's proposed examination problem: understanding material knowledge or Knowledge Corruption may require first encountering or ingesting the very poisoned material under examination.

Here, *ingest* is retained from the author's conceptual definition. This page does **not** establish that ingestion is universally necessary, harmless, or sufficient for understanding, and does not recommend performing an unsafe operation.

The defining issue is the asserted dependency of **inspection upon exposure to the subject being inspected**. Whether a particular case demonstrates this dependency remains an open evidentiary question.

## Relationships

- **[Knowledge Corruption](../knowledge-corruption/README.md):** A condition the author identifies as potentially requiring exposure for understanding; necessity remains unproven.
- **[Poison Cache](../poison-cache/README.md):** A separate concept rooted in Knowledge Corruption; no identity or necessary sequence is presumed.
- **[Designed-for-Rejection](../designed-for-rejection/README.md):** A separate rejection-oriented proposed mechanism; no necessary equivalence is asserted.

## Status and limits

**Status:** Author-defined research vocabulary; proposed epistemic problem, not a proven universal requirement or operational capability. The classification does not by itself authorize exposure, adoption, propagation, or deployment of harmful material.
