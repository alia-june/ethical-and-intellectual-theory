# Life Inventory

**Public blank-materials edition · 2026-11-28**  
**Project status:** `PROPOSED`  
**Approved for live member intake:** **no**

Life Inventory is an optional, paced, member-controlled set of blank materials for preserving a DMH clubhouse member's chosen account of life, voice, presence, history, arts, works, embodied experience, knowledge, and hope.

The member-directed preservation frame is:

> `persona alma historia artes operae corpus et scientia spesque`

The result is a partial, purpose-bound life inventory—not an exhaustive biography, a complete model of a person, or authority to speak for the member.

These materials are a personal public proposal. They are not an official statement, approved program, or policy of the Massachusetts Department of Mental Health, Clubhouse International, any clubhouse, or any member.

## Member authority

The participating member remains the primary authority over meaning, inclusion, correction, pacing, custody choice, retention choice, and proposed release, subject to applicable law and previously explained limits.

- participation, refusal, skipping, pausing, and withdrawal must be consequence-free;
- no silence, fatigue, absence, or failure to answer is acceptance;
- original words remain distinguishable from facilitator notes and editorial drafts;
- the member may choose `ACCEPT`, `CORRECT`, `REJECT`, or `DEFER` for each reviewed section;
- no release is the default;
- every proposed disclosure requires a specific recipient, purpose, package, duration, and current member authorization; and
- publication or delivery does not make the recipient an authority over the member.

## Safety and non-claims

This package is not:

- therapy, diagnosis, crisis care, clinical assessment, or a treatment record;
- a competency, credibility, eligibility, risk, personality, or identity test;
- proof that a memory is historically exact;
- identity authentication from language, voice, behavior, or memory;
- a digital person, agent, voice clone, avatar, memorial bot, or substitute decision-maker;
- legal advice, a will, an advance directive, or a medical record; or
- a guarantee of confidentiality, deletion, security, emotional comfort, or future readability.

The system—not the member—is deliberately capability-restricted. `xOSG-0` is a project-local workflow label shown only as small provenance or release-control text where needed. It is not presented as a recovered xOS implementation, a clinical system, a security product, or demonstrated identity-preservation technology.

## Before any live use

A clubhouse must not begin member intake from these files alone. A concrete deployment still requires:

1. review and correction by a member advisory group;
2. a named human authority and responsible custodian;
3. selection and testing of a separate access-controlled record system;
4. current privacy, information-security, accessibility, policy, and legal review;
5. disclosed confidentiality limits, reporting duties, retention rules, deletion limits, complaint rights, and incident procedures;
6. facilitator training and supported-choice accommodations; and
7. fictional-data rehearsal, recovery and release testing, and a named, dated local approval.

## Public-repository rule

Do not commit completed forms, recordings, transcripts, photographs, identifiers, contact information, releases, clinical information, or member records to this repository. A participating clubhouse must establish a separate controlled record location before collecting anything from a member.

Public availability is not consent to profile a member, reconstruct a person, train a model, imitate speech, or reuse material for another purpose.

## AI boundary

The blank workflow and publications were developed with AI assistance. No live member content was used. Live member-content processing defaults to AI off. Any future proposal to use AI for transcription, editing, or another bounded task requires separate disclosure, a comparable non-AI option, specific revocable member authorization, human review, and documented provider, network, retention, training, logging, and deletion terms.

## Validation and proof boundary

The source project is attached to MAGE Core `0.1.7-prealpha` under the `OSG-3` environment contract. That attachment supplies provenance and authority boundaries; it does not give the workflow autonomous authority.

At publication:

- the MAGE descriptor and pinned core validated without warnings or errors;
- the Member Book and Stewardship Guide DOCX accessibility audits reported zero high, medium, or low findings;
- DOCX table geometry checks passed;
- all 27 Member Book pages and all 43 Stewardship Guide pages were inspected after Microsoft Word rendering; and
- the PDFs were tagged Letter documents with no encryption, form, or JavaScript, and required text was extractable.

Those checks do not establish clinical appropriateness, legal compliance, emotional safety for every participant, security in an undeclared record system, approval by an institution, or readiness for live intake.
