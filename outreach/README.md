# Outreach — staging

**Status:** Draft / review only. This section is not approved for promotion to `main` or for deployment.

This directory stages voluntary, consent-first guidance for researchers, software developers, and communities interacting with AI-assisted development. It is a place to discuss objections, alternatives, research methods, and correction—not to collect other people's work.

## Sections

- **[ReShade — Before You Use AI](reshade/README.md)**: The draft guidance page, beginning with reasons *not* to use AI, followed by alternatives, objections, research boundaries, and a voluntary place for participants in the ecosystem.
- **[ReShade standalone webpage](reshade/index.html)**: Accessible, self-contained HTML preview for a potential later publication.
- **[ReShade review checklist](reshade/REVIEW_NOTES.md)**: Editorial, permission, provenance, and publication gates.

## Governance and provenance

The parent repository's [self-doctrine](../research-ethics/SELF_DOCTRINE_EXTENDED.md) and [self-reported errata](../ERRATA.md) remain the sources for the author's ethical positions and retrospective record. This outreach section expresses a **proposed application**, not an independently attested account of other participants.

Research participation is voluntary. No third party is represented as an adviser, sponsor, critic, or participant without their permission. A public code license, an invitation, a link to private research records, and permission to publish those records are four different things.

**Release boundary:** All documents on this branch are staging drafts. No production release, GitHub Pages deployment, invitations, or merging is authorized merely by their creation.
