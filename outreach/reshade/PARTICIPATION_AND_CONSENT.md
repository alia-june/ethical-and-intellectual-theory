# ReShade outreach — voluntary participation and consent

**Status:** Proposed procedure, staging only. This document is not an agreement, a completed consent record, legal advice, or permission to access anyone's material.

## Rule at the threshold

**Access is not consent. A discussion is not approval. A license is not blanket authorization to profile a person.** Safety and applicable law remain independent constraints.

This proposed outreach is not an official ReShade program. It does not obligate any shader author, community member, or critic to engage. **No response means no participation.** An express refusal ends that proposed line of engagement; do not reopen it by switching channels or intermediaries.

## Before asking someone to review anything

1. Prepare a plain-language, one-paragraph description of the research question and why a person is being invited.
2. Confirm that an invitation is welcome and proportionate. Avoid repeated outreach to someone who has declined or asked for the discussion to stop.
3. Identify every item that might be shared. Use operator-owned experimental artifacts where possible.
4. Screen for third-party code, private correspondence, identifiable information, confidential logs, and records whose dissemination is not authorized. **Redact or exclude them before sharing.**
5. Prefer ordinary paper documentation, reproducible tests, static results, and human dialogue over sending private records or source into an LLM.
6. Determine whether the proposed review needs a link at all. A link marked 'private' may still be forwarded, copied, or retained; do not mistake obscurity for access control.

## Proposed consent record — blank template

**This is deliberately unfilled. Do not treat it as permission until the person has affirmatively agreed.**

| Field | Description to complete before any transfer |
| --- | --- |
| Record ID / version / date | Unique reference, version, and date of the actual agreement |
| Participants | Who is offering the material and who is authorized to review it |
| Purpose | The specific research question and intended outcome |
| Materials | Exact documents, snippets, data, or links covered |
| Exclusions | Third-party source, private messages, identifying data, personal profiles, or other excluded material |
| Methods | Whether human reading, compilers, static checks, local scripts, or any model processing is allowed |
| Outputs | Notes, criticism, tests, quotations, copies, derived artifacts, and whether each may be retained |
| Recipients | Who can access outputs and whether onward sharing is allowed |
| Retention | Storage destination, access limits, expected retention window, and deletion process |
| Withdrawal | How to stop prospective use, request correction, and learn the limits of deleting already-shared copies |
| Publicity | Whether the person's name, contribution, quote, or involvement may be publicly mentioned |
| Consent evidence | Explicit approval of this exact scope; do not infer from silence |
| Scope changes | New opt-in required for additional materials, methods, recipients, retention, or uses |

## Review sequence

**Draft → Invite (if welcome) → Explicit opt-in → Scoped access → Feedback → Correction / closure.**

At every step, log only what is necessary. Never record a person's refusal as participation. Do not publish submissions or identify reviewers by default. Do not interpret a critique as consent to publish it.

### Withdrawal and redress

When withdrawal or an unauthorized disclosure is reported, pause new use of affected materials; review the actual scope and existing dependencies; correct or remove material within the researcher's authority; document unresolved copies, limitations, and any necessary redress without reproducing protected information. Withdrawal cannot guarantee deletion of copies already obtained by others.

A later agreement must not be presented as consent that existed earlier.

## Disclosure discipline

- This public repository must **not** contain private chat exports, access tokens, nonpublic source code, or personally identifying records offered for review.
- Public issue threads are not suitable for sharing confidential details.
- A staging branch in a public repository is still public.
- No invitation, outreach message, private link, or server has been created by this document.
