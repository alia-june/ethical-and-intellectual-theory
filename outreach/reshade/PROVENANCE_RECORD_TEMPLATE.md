## E. Decomposting — transfer without personal profiling

**Intended cycle:** Decompose → Study → Reason → Learn → Respect provenance → Correct → Regrow.

- **Code A:** If a third-party implementation exists, document its identity and the restrictions governing it without transferring or retaining excluded content.
- **Pattern B:** Record only legitimate, independently corroborated, general computational lessons and their evidence.
- **Code A − Pattern B:** This is an exclusion boundary, **not** a request to compute or archive any identifiable remainder.
- **Researcher's own artifacts:** When examining experimental shader revisions, distinguish observations about the development process from claims about anyone's innate or stable intellectual method.
- **Similarity:** Note supported similarities without automatically claiming copying, independence, or author identity.
