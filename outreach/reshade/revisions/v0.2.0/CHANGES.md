## Why create another version?

The original drafts are evidence of the researcher's then-current intent, not throwaway intermediates. This edition records a **later** proposed clarification so the history of change remains visible without retroactively correcting the past.

| Category | Original staged formulation | Proposed clarification in v0.2.0 | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy | “Staging only” / “not published” | Explicitly “publicly readable staging; not deployed or merged” | The repository is public; staging is a production gate, not confidentiality |
| Authority | “We object…” | “This research program objects…” | Do not ascribe positions to ReShade or named developers |
| Participation | Invitation to a private review | Explicit opt-in, scope, recipient, retention, revocation boundaries | An invitation is not permission or endorsement |
| Research process | Own shaders and error study | Linear development cycle tied to documentation gap and test records | Explain why shaders were created as experimental instruments |
| Decomposting | Code A / Pattern B example | Clarify that excluded residue must never be calculated or archived | An exclusion boundary must not become a fingerprinting recipe |
| Claims | Model, tests, ethics discussed together | Separate declared objective, observed execution, evidence, and attestation | Prevent self-report from being mistaken for verification |
| Non-disruption | “Non-displacing” intention | Explicit design goal, not empirically verified outcome | Avoid ungrounded operational claims |
| Corrections | Retrospective account | Direct link to original self-reported errata | Preserve authorial accountability and chronology |

## What this edition does NOT authorize

No merge to `main`; no site deployment; no issues or invitations sent to third parties; no model conversation shares; no external source inspection; no private source or contact data uploaded; no representation of ReShade, Pascal, CeeJay, or other persons.

## Open editorial decisions

- Does the operator approve first-person phrasing and scope?
- Is “decomposting” sufficiently clear as **ethics and methodology**, rather than a technical permission grant?
- Which operator-owned test cases may safely illustrate the claim?
- Is an eventual page preferable to a moderated discussion space, and who would administer access and retention?
- Which test results, if any, may be **attested** rather than merely described?

## Archive rule

Retain the original outreach drafts with their original commit and blob identities; this new edition is additive. Future changes require another dated entry. The archive may be redacted when legal, consent, or safety obligations require it, with the reason and limits documented.
