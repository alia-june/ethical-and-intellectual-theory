## Repository priorities

The repository applies the following order:

1. **Consent and human authority** — Access, observation, attribution, and preservation do not create unlimited permission.
2. **Evidence and provenance** — Declarations, observations, inferences, corroboration, attestation, and implementation retain different statuses.
3. **Correction and uncertainty** — Unknown and contested claims remain visible. Correction does not silently erase prior states.
4. **Attribution and conceptual debt** — Human direction, AI transformation, source influence, and adoption are represented separately.
5. **Bounded construction** — Proposed methods and architectures do not acquire implementation or production status by being documented.
6. **Public release discipline** — Private material remains outside the public boundary. Each release carries its own purpose, evidence, and limits.

The **AI Collaboration Attribution Protocol** records the human author's intent and editorial decisions, the AI collaborator's reported contribution, and the material actually adopted.

This README refactor is a human-directed decomposition and AI-assisted editorial transformation. It reorganizes existing public material and establishes no independent authorship, verification, consent, or authority over the linked primary documents.

### Programming doctrine

The programming doctrine connects purpose, meaning, decomposition, explicit contracts, construction, code, and bounded evidence. Human-authored theory and human-composed decomposition remain distinct from AI-assisted formulation, mappings, automation, and finite checks.

Its anonymous presentation does not imply personal identity, universal adoption, or runtime authority.
